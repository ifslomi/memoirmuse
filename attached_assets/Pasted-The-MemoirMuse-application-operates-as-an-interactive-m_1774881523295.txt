The MemoirMuse application operates as an interactive mobile platform that combines Augmented Reality (AR), multimedia content, and gamification to deliver an immersive historical experience. The system is designed to guide users through the life and contributions of Pedro S. Tolentino using real-world interaction and digital enhancements.
When the user opens the application, they are first presented with a main interface that includes options such as Start Tour, Scan AR, Quizzes, Achievements, and Settings. Users may log in or proceed as guests depending on the system design. Once inside, they can choose to explore different features of the app.
The core functionality of the application is the AR scanning feature. Using the smartphone’s camera, users can scan specific AR markers (such as images, posters, or museum displays) placed in designated locations like museums or historical sites. Once a marker is detected, the system uses computer vision and AR technology to recognize the image and overlay digital content on the screen. This content may include 3D models, animations, text descriptions, images, and audio narration related to Pedro S. Tolentino. For example, scanning a marker may display a 3D model of a historical artifact or show a short animated story about an important event in his life.
In addition to marker-based AR, the application may also include location-based features. By using GPS, the app can identify the user’s location and unlock relevant content when they are near specific historical sites. This allows users to experience a guided tour where information automatically appears based on their surroundings.
The app also integrates gamification elements to increase engagement. As users explore and interact with AR content, they can answer quizzes, complete challenges, and earn points or badges. These rewards encourage continuous use of the application and make learning more enjoyable. Progress tracking features allow users to see completed tasks, scores, and achievements.
All multimedia content, including historical documents, images, and audio, is stored in a database or cloud storage and is accessed by the app when needed. This ensures that the information is organized, updated, and easily retrievable. Some features may work offline, while others (such as updates or additional content) require an internet connection.
From a technical perspective, the application relies on mobile development frameworks and AR technologies (such as ARCore or similar tools) to handle object recognition, motion tracking, and rendering of digital elements in real time. The system processes camera input, detects markers or environments, and accurately places virtual objects on the screen.
Overall, MemoirMuse works by blending the physical and digital worlds. Users interact with real locations or markers, and the app enhances these experiences with rich digital content. This creates an engaging, educational, and interactive way of learning history, making it more accessible and meaningful for users.

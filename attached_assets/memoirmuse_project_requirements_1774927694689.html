# MemoirMuse: Product Requirements Document ## Project Overview MemoirMuse is an interactive mobile platform combining Augmented Reality (AR), multimedia content, and gamification to deliver an immersive historical experience centered on the life and contributions of Pedro S. Tolentino. ## Core Features * **AR Scanning:** Marker-based AR to trigger 3D models, animations, and narrations. * **Location-Based Discovery:** GPS-triggered historical content and guided tours. * **Gamification:** Quizzes, challenges, points, and achievement badges. * **Multimedia Integration:** Database-backed 3D models, audio, and historical documents. * **User Management:** Account creation and login for progress tracking. ## Visual Direction * **Aesthetic:** Modern, sleek, "Pokemon Go" inspired UI. * **Key UI Element:** A bottom navigation bar with a large, highlighted central "Camera/Scan" button. * **Theme:** Tech-forward but accessible, blending physical and digital worlds. ## User Flow 1. **Onboarding:** Create account or login. 2. **Home/Dashboard:** Access Tour, Scan, Quizzes, Achievements, and Settings. 3. **Discovery:** Use the AR scanner on museum markers or reach GPS locations. 4. **Interaction:** View 3D models, listen to narrations, and complete quizzes. 5. **Progression:** Earn badges and track historical discovery progress.

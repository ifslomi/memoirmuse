# Design System Specification: The Chronos Interface

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
### Creative North Star: "The Digital Archaeologist"
This design system serves as a sophisticated lens, bridging the gap between raw historical data and high-fidelity augmented reality. It rejects the "flatness" of standard mobile apps in favor of an immersive, layered environment. 

By utilizing **intentional asymmetry** and **overlapping glass surfaces**, we create a UI that feels like a floating HUD (Heads-Up Display) rather than a static document. The layout avoids rigid grids to mimic the organic discovery of artifacts, using high-contrast typography scales (`display-lg` vs. `label-sm`) to create an editorial cadence that feels both prestigious and high-tech.

---

## 2. Colors & Surface Philosophy
The palette is rooted in the depth of the night (`surface: #131313`), allowing vibrant "digital" light and "historical" warmth to pop with cinematic intensity.

### The "No-Line" Rule
**Explicit Instruction:** Traditional 1px solid borders are strictly prohibited for sectioning. Boundaries must be defined solely through background color shifts or tonal transitions.
- Use `surface-container-low` for large background sections.
- Use `surface-container-high` or `highest` for nested interactive elements.
- This creates a seamless, "molded" look rather than a partitioned one.

### Surface Hierarchy & Nesting
Treat the UI as a series of physical layers—like stacked sheets of sapphire glass. 
- **Base Level:** `surface` (#131313).
- **Secondary Level:** `surface-container` (#201f1f) for main content blocks.
- **Interactive Level:** `surface-container-highest` (#353534) for buttons or active states.

### The "Glass & Gradient" Rule
To achieve a premium AR feel, all floating panels must utilize **Glassmorphism**:
- **Background:** `surface-variant` (#353534) at 60% opacity.
- **Effect:** `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
- **Accent:** Use a subtle linear gradient from `primary` (#c3f5ff) to `primary-container` (#00e5ff) at a 45-degree angle for primary actions to give them a "powered-on" glow.

---

## 3. Typography
The system utilizes a dual-font strategy to balance futuristic precision with modern readability.

*   **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** A tech-forward, geometric sans-serif. Used for high-impact moments, artifact titles, and AR markers. The wide apertures and mono-linear weights suggest a digital terminal.
*   **Body & Labels (Manrope):** A highly legible, modern sans-serif. Used for historical descriptions and functional UI labels. It provides a warm, human touch to balance the coldness of the tech.

**Hierarchy as Identity:** 
- Use `display-lg` for "Aha!" moments (e.g., discovering a new era).
- Use `label-sm` with increased letter spacing (0.05rem) for metadata to mimic high-end watch face detailing.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth
In this system, elevation is conveyed through **Tonal Layering** rather than structural shadows.

### The Layering Principle
Depth is achieved by "stacking" the surface-container tiers. Place a `surface-container-lowest` card on a `surface-container-low` section to create a soft, natural "recessed" effect.

### Ambient Shadows
When a floating HUD element requires a lift:
- **Shadow Color:** A tinted version of `on-surface` (e.g., #e5e2e1 at 5% opacity).
- **Blur:** Extra-diffused (Blur: 40px, Spread: 0). This mimics the soft glow of a screen in a dark room rather than a harsh drop shadow.

### The "Ghost Border" Fallback
If an edge must be defined for accessibility, use the **Ghost Border**:
- **Token:** `outline-variant` (#3b494c).
- **Opacity:** 15%.
- **Result:** A whisper of a line that suggests a physical edge without breaking the immersion.

---

## 5. Components

### Primary AR Action Button
- **Background:** Gradient of `primary` to `primary-container`.
- **Shape:** `rounded-full` (9999px) for a "tactile stone" feel.
- **Typography:** `title-md` (Manrope), Bold.
- **Interaction:** On press, scale to 95% and increase `surface-tint` glow.

### Achievement Cards (The "Relic" Style)
- **Background:** `tertiary-container` (#ffc948) at 10% opacity.
- **Edge:** 1px Ghost Border using `tertiary` (#ffebc6) at 20%.
- **Highlight:** Use `tertiary-fixed-dim` (#fabd00) for icons to represent gold historical significance.

### Navigation "Pulse" Chips
- **States:** 
    - *Inactive:* `surface-container-highest` with `on-surface-variant` text.
    - *Active:* `primary-container` background with `on-primary-container` text.
- **Shape:** `rounded-md` (1.5rem).

### Lists & Collections
**No Dividers.** Separate list items using `spacing-4` (1rem) of vertical white space or a subtle background shift to `surface-container-low`.

### AR HUD Tooltips
- **Visuals:** High glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur: 12px`).
- **Corner Radius:** `rounded-sm` (0.5rem).
- **Positioning:** Always anchored with a subtle `primary` color pulse to indicate the "tracked" object in 3D space.

---

## 6. Do’s and Don’ts

### Do:
*   **Use Asymmetry:** Place titles off-center or allow images to bleed off the edge of `surface-container` units to create a dynamic, "un-templated" feel.
*   **Embrace High Contrast:** Pair `display-lg` headings with `body-sm` metadata to create a professional, editorial hierarchy.
*   **Leverage Negative Space:** Use `spacing-12` (3rem) and `spacing-16` (4rem) to let historical artifacts "breathe."

### Don’t:
*   **Don't use pure black (#000000):** It kills the depth. Always use the deep indigo/charcoal of `surface` (#131313).
*   **Don't use 100% opaque borders:** This makes the UI feel like a legacy web form. Use background shifts instead.
*   **Don't crowd the AR View:** Keep functional UI to the top and bottom thirds of the screen, leaving the center clear for the immersive experience.
*   **Don't use standard "Grey" shadows:** Always tint your shadows with the primary or secondary accent colors to maintain the "light-emitting" digital aesthetic.
